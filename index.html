Simulation

How it's computed

Each hour, the markets clear in the order of the rows. An accepted order is added to unit.outputs[product_type], as set_dispatch_plan does. Before every market the tool calls ASSUME's functions on the outputs so far.

Power plant

  • calculate_min_max_power: max = availability·max_power − capacity_pos − energy, min = min_power + capacity_neg − energy, both clipped at 0.
  • calculate_ramp(op_time, previous_power, power, current_power) applies ramp_up, ramp_down, min_operating_time and min_down_time.
  • op_time comes from get_operation_time on the previous hours.

Storage

  • calculate_min_max_discharge / _charge subtract the sold energy and the reserves, then clip by SOC.
  • calculate_ramp_discharge / _charge limit the change from the previous hour.
  • SOC carries over using set_dispatch_plan, or execute_current_dispatch when that option is on.

Orders

  • Type a volume, or max for the volume the flexable strategies would bid (-max for the lowest energy volume).
  • Click ✓ to mark an order as not accepted.
