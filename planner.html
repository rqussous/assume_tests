    How it's computed

    Markets clear one after another in the order above. Each accepted bid becomes a commitment that later markets have to respect.

    Thermal unit

    • Ramp window at step t: U = min(Pmax, Pt−1 + ramp↑), L = max(Pmin, Pt−1 − ramp↓). From standstill, U = min(Pmax, max(Pmin, ramp↑)).
    • Energy bids move the operating point P. Positive volumes sell, negative volumes buy back.
    • Reserve bids must stay deliverable: P + R↑ ≤ U and P − R↓ ≥ L.
    • If reserves clear before energy, the unit gets a must-run obligation of L + R↓.

    Storage

    • Net power P runs from −charge max to +discharge max. Positive means discharging.
    • SOCt = SOCt−1 − P·Δt/ηdis when discharging and − P·Δt·ηch when charging.
    • Reserves hold back energy for their activation duration: R↑·d/ηdis above SOC min and R↓·d·ηch below capacity.

    Bids

    • Type a volume, or max to offer everything still available (-max buys back or charges as much as possible).
    • Click the outcome button to switch between accepted, partial and rejected.
    • Output and SOC carry into the next step. Reserves are assumed not to be activated.
